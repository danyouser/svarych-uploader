Privacy Policy — SVARYCH uploader

Effective date: 20 November 2026

SVARYCH uploader ("the tool") is a private command-line tool used only by Bohdan Lukach, the owner of the YouTube channel SVARYCH, to publish his own videos to his own channel. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

What data the tool accesses

  • Google OAuth 2.0 tokens issued when the channel owner signs in, with the scopes needed to upload and manage videos on his channel (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl).
  • Channel and video information of the owner's own channel: channel ID and title, and the IDs, titles, descriptions, privacy and processing status of the videos the tool uploads.
  • Files the owner chooses to upload: his videos, thumbnails and subtitles, read from his own computer.

The tool does not access, collect or store any data of other YouTube users, does not read comments, subscribers' personal data or analytics of other channels, and does not use cookies or tracking of any kind.

How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's videos and set their details (title, description, thumbnail, subtitles, audience and AI disclosure settings) on his own channel, and to check that the upload succeeded. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth tokens and the tool's log of uploads are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder that is not published or synced to any public service.
  • No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party. Data is sent only to Google / YouTube through YouTube API Services to perform the uploads.
  • No server of ours receives the data; the tool has no backend.

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept only while the owner uses the tool. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the tool can no longer access the channel, and the local token file is deleted.
  • To have any data held by the tool deleted, open an issue; it is deleted within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
