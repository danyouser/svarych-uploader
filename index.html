SVARYCH uploader

A private publishing tool of the music channel SVARYCH on YouTube.

SVARYCH uploader is a small command-line tool that the channel owner, Bohdan Lukach, runs on his own computer to publish his own music videos and Shorts to his own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services.

What it does

  • uploads the owner's finished videos to the owner's channel, with title, description, category, language, the "not made for kids" setting and the altered or synthetic content (AI) disclosure;
  • sets the owner's custom thumbnails and subtitle files on those videos;
  • reads the processing status of the uploaded videos.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone, has no public sign-up and no web interface. It signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 and works only with the channel the owner chooses when he signs in.

Data

The tool accesses no data of other YouTube users. How the owner's own data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
